Direction

One idea, never explained

The viewer watches the badge get built, piece by piece, before anyone names it. By the time it reads Trusted Agent, they've seen why.

One camera, one take

No pages and no snapping. Every scene hands off to the next inside the same world, and the camera never cuts unless it passes through black.

Every object carries meaning

The doorway in Chapter 01 is the badge's arch with its keystone missing. Nobody notices until Chapter 05, and that's the point.

Purple is earned too

Purple appears only when something becomes meaningful, seven times in the whole film. Everything else is stone, bone and shadow.

Type is a physical object

Headlines sit at real depths in the scene. The camera moves past them, and sometimes through them.

Two directions

Structural Recommended

Archivo has a width axis that runs from 62% to 125%. Type can stretch and compress as the camera moves, which handles the brief's kinetic letter-spacing without faking it.

  • One family covers the hairline "is" and the heavy headlines
  • The wide setting reads as architectural
  • Renders as real 3D text in the scene

Monumental

Tall and condensed, like signage on a building. Long words like EVERYONE and CONSISTENT can fill the full screen height without breaking.

  • No width axis, so kinetic effects rely on scale and tracking
  • Closer to an event poster than a technology brand

Motion & sound

Weight and inertia

The camera behaves like it's on a dolly: it has mass, it eases into moves, and it never jumps to the scroll position.

  • Damped cameraThe camera chases the scroll position through a spring. Stopping the scroll lets it settle over about half a second.
  • Scroll speedFast scrolling widens the lens up to 3° and loosens type tracking slightly. Both relax within 600 ms. Holds compress but are never skipped.
  • CursorThe badge tilts up to 6° toward the pointer. Nearby figures and dust drift away from it. All of it should feel subconscious.
  • Reduced motionCamera moves become crossfades between these still frames. No lens or speed effects. The story stays the same.

What makes an agent trusted?

The "See how trust is earned" button leads here. Each badge part maps to one product dimension. It shows no scores, only what each one means. Select a dimension to see its part.
